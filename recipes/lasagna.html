Lasagna

Description

This classic homemade lasagna is the ultimate comfort food.

Layered with rich, slow-simmered beef ragù, silky ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella, every bite delivers an unbeatable depth of flavor.

While preparing lasagna from scratch requires a bit of time and patience, the result is far superior to any frozen store-bought alternative.

Baked at 375°F, the top layer crisps into a beautiful golden-brown crust while keeping the center tender and juicy.

Serve hot alongside fresh garlic bread for an unforgettable family meal!

Ingredients

  • 1 Pound ground beef.
  • 1 Package lasagna noodles
  • 2 Cups ricotta cheese
  • 2 Cups mariana sauce
  • 1 Egg
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Boil the lasagna noodles according to package instructions and drain.
  2. In a skillet over medium heat, brown the ground beef and stir in the marinara sauce.
  3. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, salt, and pepper.
  4. In a baking dish, layer meat sauce, noodles, ricotta mixture, and mozzarella cheese.
  5. Repeat layers and bake at 375°F (190°C) 45 minutes until bubbly and golden.
