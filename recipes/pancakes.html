Fluffy Pancakes

Light, airy, and golden-brown pancakes made from scratch.

Served warm topped with a scoop of cold vanilla ice cream and a generous drizzle of sweet chocolate syrup.

Forget store-bought boxed mixes. Making pancake batter from scratch takes less than ten minutes and produces infinitely fluffier, tastier results.

Perfect for a cozy weekend breakfast!

Ingredients:

  • 1 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour.
  • 3 and 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon white sugar
  • 1 and 1/4 cups milk
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tablespoons melted butter
  • 2 scoop ice cream

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, sift together the flour, baking powder, salt, and sugar.
  2. Make a well in the center and pour in the milk, egg, and melted butter; mix until smooth.
  3. Heat a lightly oiled griddle or frying pan over medium-high heat.
  4. Pour or scoop the batter onto the griddle, using approximately 1/4 cup for each pancake.
  5. Cook until bubbles form on top and the edges look set, then flip and brown the other side.
  6. Transfer the warm pancakes to a plate, top with a large scoop of vanilla ice cream, and serve immediately.
