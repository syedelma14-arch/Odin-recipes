Crunchy Creamy Tacos

Delicious authentic street-style tacos packed with tender seasoned meat, fresh cilantro, diced onions, and a squeeze of lime wrapped in warm corn tortillas.

Whether prepared for a quick weeknight dinner or a weekend gathering, homemade tacos are easy, versatile, and packed with bold flavors.

Serve warm with your favorite salsa!

Ingredients:

  • 1 pound ground beef or carne asada
  • 1 tablespoon taco seasoning
  • 8 small corn tortillas
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped white onion
  • 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • 1/2 cup salsa or hot sauce

Here are the taco preparation steps formatted as point-by-point instructions:

  1. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the meat until fully browned.
  2. Stir in the taco seasoning along with a splash of water and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes.
  3. Warm the corn tortillas on a hot, dry skillet for 30 seconds on each side.
  4. Assemble the tacos by placing a spoonful of meat onto each tortilla.
  5. Top with chopped onions, fresh cilantro, a drizzle of salsa, and a squeeze of fresh lime juice.
